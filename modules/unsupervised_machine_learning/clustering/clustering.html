Clustering Algorithms

← Back to Unsupervised Learning

Centroid-Based Clustering

Partitioning data into groups based on distance to a central point (K-Means, K-Medoids).

Hierarchical Clustering (AHC)

Building a hierarchy of clusters in a bottom-up or top-down fashion.

Density-Based Clustering

Grouping points in dense regions, separated by sparse areas (DBSCAN, OPTICS).

Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM)

A probabilistic model assuming data is from a mixture of Gaussian distributions.
