1. The Core Idea: Finding and Classifying Nouns

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is a subtask of information extraction that seeks to locate and classify named entities in unstructured text into pre-defined categories such as person names, organizations, locations, medical codes, time expressions, quantities, monetary values, percentages, etc.

2. How it Works: From Words to Spans

NER models are typically trained on a large corpus of text that has been manually annotated with entity labels. The model learns to predict the probability that a given word or sequence of words (a "span") belongs to a particular entity class. Modern NER systems often use transformer-based architectures to achieve high accuracy.

3. Applications of NER

  • Information Extraction: Automatically populating databases with information extracted from documents.
  • Content Recommendation: Recommending articles or products based on the entities mentioned in a user's reading history.
  • Customer Support: Automatically routing customer support tickets based on the products or locations mentioned.
  • Healthcare: Extracting information about diseases, drugs, and symptoms from clinical notes.

4. Conclusion

NER is a fundamental building block for many NLP applications. It provides a way to extract structured information from unstructured text, which can then be used to power a wide range of downstream tasks. The ability to automatically identify key entities in a text is a crucial step towards understanding its meaning and content.
